swami vivekananda

swami Vivekananda 1863 born Narendranath Datta,[b] was an Indian Hindu monk, philosopher, author, religious teacher, and the chief disciple of the Indian mystic Ramakrishna.[4][5] Vivekananda was a major figure in the introduction of Vedanta and Yoga to the Western world during the late 19th century,[6][7][8] and is credited with raising interfaith awareness and elevating Hinduism to the status of a major world religion.[9]
