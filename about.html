1.Early Years (1916–1939)


  • Aircraft Roots: Formed out of Rapp Motorenwerke and Bayerische Flugzeugwerke (BFW), BMW initially produced aircraft engines, including the BMW IIIa during World War I.



  • Transition to Automobiles: Following WWI restrictions, BMW shifted to History of BMW - Wikipedia farm equipment, household items, and motorcycles, eventually entering car production in 1928 by acquiring Fahrzeugfabrik Eisenach (producing the Dixi 3/15).



  • Iconic Design: The BMW 303 in 1933 introduced BMW's signature kidney grille, followed by the renowned 326 and 328 sports



    •  2. Post-WWII Struggle and Survival (1945–1959)


  • Wartime Destruction: Factories were heavily bombed, and operations in East Germany fell under Soviet control (later producing cars under the EMW brand).



  • Products: BMW temporarily manufactured kitchen pots, pans, and bicycles to survive, before launching its first post-war luxury car, the BMW 501 ("Baroque Angel") in 1952, and licensing the Isetta bubble car.



  • The 1959 Rescue: Nearing bankruptcy, the company was saved from a takeover by Daimler-Benz thanks to a massive financial investment by the Quandt family and the success of the BMW 70
